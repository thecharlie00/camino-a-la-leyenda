Privacy and cookies policy – Futbol: Road to Glory

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy applies to the Android app Futbol: Road to Glory (com.caminoleyenda.app) published on Google Play and to the website futbolroadtolegend.com, both developed and published by Carlos Sánchez Abad.

Summary

The Futbol: Road to Glory website is free with third-party ads from bauval.org, which load only if you accept optional services. Google Analytics is also enabled only with your consent. We do not sell your data and you do not need an account to play.

What the game stores

The names you type, your saves, your chosen language and the Hall of Fame are kept in your browser's local storage. They are not sent automatically; only the information described under GOAT LADDER is shared if you choose to publish a record.

GOAT LADDER

GOAT LADDER is optional. When you open the ranking, Cloudflare processes the request and may receive your IP address. Only after you press Publish career and accept are your alias, title, mode, current club, seasons, aggregate metrics and season-by-season history (clubs, competitions, stats, trophies and awards) sent; this information becomes public. Individual achievements can also be published. Your profile name and full save are not sent. The data is client-reported and is not verified by a game server. Do not include personal information in your alias or title. You can delete a publication from the same device while its local data is retained; do so before clearing it.

Android app (Futbol: Road to Glory)

The Android app Futbol: Road to Glory (com.caminoleyenda.app) stores your games, names, language and Hall of Fame locally and does not send them automatically. It requests internet access for update checks and, if you choose, GOAT LADDER publishing. The Android app integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): interstitial ads may appear only on a return-to-menu transition, never during matches. Google and its partners may process IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions and diagnostics to deliver, limit, measure and protect ads; personalization depends on consent and applicable law. Google UMP manages consent, and you can revisit choices under Privacy options in the app. See Google's Privacy Policy. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes local data. App-specific policy: https://futbolroadtolegend.com/privacidad/app/

Ads and third-party cookies

Website ads are served through third-party scripts from bauval.org. They load only after you accept optional services in the privacy notice. These providers may receive technical browser data and use cookies or identifiers under their own notices and policies; we do not control their practices or confirm their retention periods. Ads never appear inside gameplay or during matches; the play page shows only a banner outside the game area.

Visit statistics (Google Analytics)

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see how it is used: which pages you open, which site you came from, how long you stay, your approximate country or city and your type of device, browser and operating system. To tell visits apart it stores the _ga and _ga_LGPGHRGSSC cookies, which last up to 2 years. Google Analytics does not log or store your IP address. This data is not combined with your saves or with GOAT LADDER and is not used to identify you. Google processes it on our behalf and may process it outside the European Union with the safeguards described in its policy. The Android app does not use Google Analytics.

  • How Google protects Analytics data: support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245
  • You can block Google Analytics on every site with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Your consent

The site asks before loading optional ads from bauval.org or Google Analytics. If you reject, those services are not loaded and you can keep using the site. You can change your choice at any time using the “Privacy and cookies” link in the footer of any page.

Deleting your data

You can delete any save from “Load game” and your records with “Delete my record” in GOAT LADDER. Clearing this site's browser data removes the local key needed to remove public entries, so delete the record first. To remove advertising data held by third parties, consult their policies and clear this site's cookies in your browser. Google Analytics cookies are stored only if you accepted that service.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security reasons. More information in the GitHub privacy statement.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Player names

Footballer and manager names are made-up versions inspired by real football; the game is not affiliated with any club, league or player.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, you will see it here with the update date. For any question or request about your data (for example, deleting a GOAT LADDER entry), contact Carlos Sánchez Abad at carlossancheztecno@gmail.com.
