Privacy and cookies

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Summary

The Camino a la Leyenda website is free thanks to Google AdSense ads, which appear on the page around the game and never inside a match. We don't sell your data and you don't need an account to play.

What the game stores

The names you type, your saves, your chosen language and the Hall of Fame are kept in your browser's local storage. They are not sent automatically; only the information described under GOAT LADDER is shared if you choose to publish a record.

GOAT LADDER

GOAT LADDER is optional. When you open the ranking, Cloudflare processes the request and may receive your IP address. Only after you press Publish career and accept are your alias, title, mode, current club, seasons, aggregate metrics and season-by-season history (clubs, competitions, stats, trophies and awards) sent; this information becomes public. Individual achievements can also be published. Your profile name and full save are not sent. The data is client-reported and is not verified by a game server. Do not include personal information in your alias or title. You can delete a publication from the same device while its local data is retained; do so before clearing it.

Ads and third-party cookies

This website uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its advertising partners use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites, and to measure how they perform. To do so they may collect your IP address, approximate location, technical browser data and information about how you interact with ads.

  • How Google uses data from its partners' websites: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • You can turn off personalised ads at myadcenter.google.com, and those from other providers at aboutads.info.

Your consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the site asks when you arrive whether you accept cookies and personalised ads. If you don't, the ads you see won't be personalised. You can change your choice at any time with the “Privacy and cookies” link at the bottom of any page.

Deleting your data

You can delete any save from “Load game” and your records with “Delete my record” in GOAT LADDER. Clearing this site's browser data removes the local key needed to remove public entries, so delete the record first. To delete the advertising data Google holds, go to myadcenter.google.com.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security reasons. More information in the GitHub privacy statement.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Player names

Footballer and manager names are made-up versions inspired by real football; the game is not affiliated with any club, league or player.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, you'll see it here with the update date. For questions about the game, visit the project page.
