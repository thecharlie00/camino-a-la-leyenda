Choose how to play

Three ways to start. You can keep several saves at once and switch between them whenever you like.

  • Player career

    Create your footballer and live his whole career, from the academy to retirement. When you hang up your boots, you can carry on as a manager.

  • Manager career

    Start in the dugout of the club you choose: signings, line-ups, renewals and dressing-room morale. Plus tactical changes mid-match.

  • Create your club

    Invent a club with its own name, colours and crest, put it in any league you like and build it up from the bottom with a new squad.

Every mode can be played in men's or women's football.

How it plays

A week of play takes a single tap. What matters, the match and the decisions, you see in your own time.

What a season brings

Leagues, cups and national teams from around the world, with made-up names inspired by real football.

Matches you can watch
The dots move according to each player's pace, passing and positioning. You take the penalties and free kicks yourself.
Decisions off the pitch
Press conferences, offers, dressing-room rows and moments that change a career.
Your transfers
Negotiate contracts, ask for a transfer or, as manager, buy and sell to balance the books.
Achievements and Hall of Fame
Dozens of achievements, trophies and records that stay with you even when you start a new save.
In five languages
Spanish, English, German, Italian and French. Switch any time from the menu.
Your save, with you
Copy your save as a code and keep playing in another browser or in the Android app.
