This policy explains what data the Android app Futbol: Road to Glory (com.caminoleyenda.app), developed and published by Carlos Sánchez Abad, uses, when any data leaves your device and how you can delete it. You do not need an account to play, and we do not sell your data.

Data stored on your device

The names you type, your saved games, your language setting and the Hall of Fame are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. They are not sent to the developer or to any server automatically.

Update check

When the app comes to the foreground it may request a small version file from futbolroadtolegend.com (hosted on GitHub Pages) to tell you whether a new version is available on Google Play. The request contains no personal or game data; as with any web request, the hosting provider (GitHub) may log your IP address for security purposes.

GOAT LADDER (optional public ranking)

You can choose to publish a career or an individual feat to the public GOAT LADDER ranking. Nothing is sent until you enter an alias and a title, review the information and confirm. For a career, the published data is your alias, title, game mode, current club, seasons played, cumulative statistics and season-by-season history (clubs, competitions, statistics, titles and awards). For a feat, your alias, title and the metrics of that performance. This information becomes public. Your profile name and your full saved game are never sent. The ranking runs on Cloudflare, which processes the request and may receive your IP address. Do not include personal data in your alias or title. You can delete your publication from the same device with “Delete my entry” while you keep the app's local data.

Game sharing code

If you ask the app to create a code to copy or share your game, that code contains your saved game only, and it is shared only if you copy and send it yourself.

Advertising and analytics

The Android app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and may show interstitial ads only on a safe return-to-menu transition, never during a match. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as your IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions and diagnostics to deliver ads, limit frequency, measure performance and prevent fraud; ad personalization is subject to your choices and applicable law. Google's UMP consent flow may appear when required, and you can revisit your choices through Privacy options in the app. Google explains its processing in its Privacy Policy and advertising information.

Permissions

The app uses internet access for update checks, GOAT LADDER when you choose to publish, and the AdMob ad and consent services described above. Where permitted, the AdMob SDK may use Android's advertising identifier and include its technical permission; this does not trigger a runtime permission prompt.

Deleting your data

You can delete any game from “Load game”. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes all locally stored data. To delete a GOAT LADDER entry, use “Delete my entry” before uninstalling, or write to the contact email above with the alias and title of the entry.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Player names

Player, manager and club names are made-up versions inspired by real football. The app is not affiliated with any club, league or player.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date. For any question or request about your data, contact Carlos Sánchez Abad at carlossancheztecno@gmail.com.
